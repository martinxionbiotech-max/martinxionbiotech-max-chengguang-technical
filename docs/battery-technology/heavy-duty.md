# Heavy Duty Automotive Battery

## 1. Definition

A **Heavy Duty** automotive battery is a thick-plate flooded lead-acid battery designed for commercial vehicles — trucks, buses, construction equipment, and agricultural machinery. It prioritizes vibration resistance, deep-cycling capability, and sustained high-current delivery over compact size.

## 2. How It Works

Heavy duty batteries use thicker positive plates (2.5-4.0mm vs 1.5-2.0mm in SLI) with lead-antimony (Pb-Sb) or lead-calcium-tin alloy grids. The thicker plates provide more active material for sustained discharge and better resistance to deep discharge damage. Higher electrolyte volume above plates reduces watering frequency.

## 3. Construction

- **Thick positive plates** (2.5-4.0mm) with Pb-Sb alloy grids for structural strength
- **Deep sump** design — extra electrolyte reservoir below plates
- **Double-sealed cover** with labyrinth venting to prevent acid leakage
- **Polypropylene ribbed container** for impact and vibration resistance
- **Heavy-duty cast-on straps (COS)** for high-current flow
- **Recessed terminals** with robust post seals

## 4. Materials

- Positive plates: Thick PbO2 paste on Pb-Sb grid
- Negative plates: Sponge lead on Pb-Sb or Pb-Ca grid
- Separator: Microporous PE with glass mat backing
- Electrolyte: 37% H2SO4 with higher specific gravity (1.280-1.300)
- Case: High-impact PP copolymer with ribbed exterior
- Intercell connectors: Lead through-the-wall or external strap

## 5. Technical Parameters

- Voltage: 12V (some 24V via dual configuration)
- CCA: 800-1,200+ A (SAE)
- Capacity: 120-225 Ah (C20)
- Cycle life: 300-500 cycles
- Vibration resistance: Exceeds SAE J537 severe duty
- Reserve Capacity: 200-400+ minutes

## 6. Advantages

- Extreme vibration resistance for off-road and heavy equipment
- High CCA for large diesel engine starting
- Deep cycle tolerance (better than SLI)
- Long service life in demanding applications
- Large electrolyte reserve reduces maintenance
- Robust terminals for high-current draw

## 7. Limitations

- Very heavy (30-55 kg per battery)
- Large physical footprint
- Higher cost than standard SLI
- Periodic water maintenance (Pb-Sb grids)
- Higher self-discharge (Pb-Sb grids)
- Not suitable for passenger vehicles

## 8. Applications

- Medium and heavy-duty trucks (Isuzu, Hino, Fuso)
- Large buses and coaches
- Construction equipment (excavators, loaders, dozers)
- Agricultural machinery (tractors, harvesters)
- Marine starting (commercial vessels)
- Diesel generators (starting)
- Mining equipment

## 10. Testing

Heavy duty batteries subjected to: SAE J537 severe vibration test (5g, 8 hours), high-rate discharge at -18degC, reserve capacity at 25A, deep cycling to 80% DoD, and thermal shock testing (-40degC to +60degC).

## 11. Manufacturing Considerations

Heavy duty battery production requires thick-grid casting equipment, extended curing cycles for thick plates, over-pasting control for uniform active material density, and specialized welding for heavy-duty intercell connections.

## 15. Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Why are heavy duty batteries so much heavier than car batteries?**

The thicker lead plates contain 2-3x more lead, providing more active material for sustained discharge and mechanical strength for vibration resistance.

**Q: Can I use a heavy duty battery in my car?**

Physically unlikely — the larger case dimensions and different terminal layout typically won't fit a passenger car battery tray.
