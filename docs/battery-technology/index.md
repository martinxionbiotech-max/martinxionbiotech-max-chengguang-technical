# SLI vs AGM vs EFB vs Heavy Duty — Complete Technology Comparison

## Quick Decision Matrix

| Your Requirement | Best Technology | Why |
|-----------------|:---:|-----|
| Lowest cost, conventional vehicle | **SLI** | $ lowest, proven, 3-5 year life |
| Start-stop vehicle (entry level) | **EFB** | 2x SLI life, moderate cost |
| Start-stop vehicle (full/aggressive) | **AGM** | 3-4x SLI life, fastest recharge |
| Premium/luxury vehicle | **AGM** | High electrical load handling |
| Truck, bus, construction equipment | **Heavy Duty** | Vibration-proof, deep cycle tolerant |
| Cold climate (-30degC or below) | **AGM** or **Heavy Duty** | Best cold CCA performance |
| Hot climate (40degC+) | **AGM** | Better heat tolerance than flooded |
| Deep cycle (accessories, camping) | **AGM** | Tolerates 50-80% discharge |
| UPS/telecom/standby | **VRLA** | Long float life, sealed |
