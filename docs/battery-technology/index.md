# Battery Technology

Understanding automotive battery technologies is essential for selecting the right battery for your market and application.

---

| Technology | Best For | Cycle Life | Cost |
|-----------|----------|:---:|:---:|
| [SLI](sli.md) | Conventional vehicles | 200-500 cycles | :material-star: Low |
| [AGM](agm.md) | Start-stop, premium | 400-800 cycles | :material-star::material-star::material-star: High |
| [EFB](efb.md) | Entry start-stop | 300-600 cycles | :material-star::material-star: Medium |
| [Heavy Duty](heavy-duty.md) | Trucks, buses, construction | 300-500 cycles | :material-star::material-star::material-star: High |
| [VRLA](vrla.md) | UPS, telecom, standby | 500-1000+ cycles | :material-star::material-star: Medium |

---

### Quick Comparison

| Feature | SLI | AGM | EFB | Heavy Duty |
|---------|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
| Electrolyte | Flooded liquid | Absorbed in glass mat | Flooded + carbon | Flooded thick |
| Sealed | No | Yes (VRLA) | No | No |
| Deep Cycle | No | Yes | Moderate | Moderate |
| Cold CCA | Good | Excellent | Good | Excellent |
