# Plate Curing

## 1. Definition

Controlled temperature and humidity process that converts paste into mechanically strong, electrochemically active material.

## 2. Process

Plates stacked in curing chambers at controlled temperature (35-85degC) and humidity (95%+ RH) for 24-72 hours. Process: free lead oxidation, tribasic lead sulfate crystal formation, grid-paste bond development. Cured plates develop characteristic chocolate-brown color.

## 3. Quality Control

Free lead content (<2%), crystal structure (3BS vs 4BS), plate strength (drop test), moisture content. Chengguang uses multi-zone curing chambers with automated control.
