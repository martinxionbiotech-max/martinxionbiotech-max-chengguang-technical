# Final Testing & Inspection

## 1. Definition

Post-formation quality verification — every battery tested before shipment.

## 2. Process

High-rate discharge test (CCA simulation), capacity verification, voltage check, visual inspection (case, terminals, labels), leak test, weight check. Defective batteries identified and segregated. IATF 16949 requires full traceability from raw materials to shipment.

## 3. Quality Control

Pass/fail criteria per IEC 60095-1, EN 50342-1, JIS D 5301. Chengguang performs 100% testing — no sampling for final QC.

## 4. Manufacturing at Chengguang

Chengguang Power Tech operates 18 automated assembly lines producing 40,000 batteries per day. Manufacturing is IATF 16949 certified with full batch traceability from raw materials to shipment.
