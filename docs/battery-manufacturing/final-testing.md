# Final Testing & Inspection

## 1. Definition

Post-formation quality verification where every single battery is tested before shipment. Chengguang performs 100% testing — no sampling-based release. Defective batteries are identified, segregated, and analyzed for root cause. This is the last quality gate before the battery leaves the factory.

## 2. Process

### 1. High-Rate Discharge Test (100%)
CCA simulation: Battery is discharged at the rated CCA current for 30 seconds at ambient temperature. Voltage must remain above the specification threshold (typically >7.2V at -18degC equivalent). This is the most critical pass/fail test.

### 2. Open Circuit Voltage Check (100%)
OCV must be >12.60V after formation and stabilization. Low OCV indicates: internal short circuit, insufficient formation, or contaminated electrolyte.

### 3. Visual Inspection (100%)
- **Case:** No cracks, no deformation, no acid residue. Color consistent.
- **Terminals:** Correct type, position, and post diameter. No damage or corrosion.
- **Label:** Correct model, specifications, polarity markings. Aligned properly. No peeling edges.
- **Cover seal:** No gaps, no electrolyte leakage. Heat seal uniform around perimeter.
- **Handle (if equipped):** Securely attached, correct color.

### 4. Leak Test (100%)
Battery is pressurized to 2-3 psi and checked for pressure drop over 30 seconds. Or: inverted and observed for electrolyte leakage. Any leakage = immediate rejection.

### 5. Weight Check (100%)
Weight must be within specification range. Underweight = missing electrolyte or plates. Overweight = potential internal defect.

### 6. Batch Sampling Tests (1 per 500-2000)
- **C20 Capacity** (IEC 60095-1): 20-hour discharge at C20 rate. Must deliver >=95% of rated Ah.
- **Reserve Capacity** (SAE J537): 25A discharge to 10.5V. Minutes recorded.
- **Charge Acceptance** (EN 50342-1): Current accepted at 14.4V from 80% SoC.
- **Vibration Test** (JIS D 5301 or EN 50342-1): 3-5g RMS for 8 hours. No damage, no leakage, CCA retention >90%.

### 7. Documentation
Every battery's test data (CCA, OCV, weight, production timestamp) is recorded and stored for 5 years. Certificate of Analysis (CoA) is generated per shipment lot. This full traceability is a core IATF 16949 requirement.

## 3. Quality Control at Chengguang

CCA (> specification), OCV (>12.60V), leak test (no pressure loss), visual (no defects), weight (within range). Chengguang: 100% testing, zero sampling-based release. IATF 16949 full traceability from grid casting to final test.
