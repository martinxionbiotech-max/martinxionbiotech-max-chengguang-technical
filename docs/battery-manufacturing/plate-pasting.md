# Plate Pasting

## 1. Definition

Application of active material paste (PbO2 for positive, Pb for negative) onto lead grids.

## 2. Process

Lead oxide powder mixed with water and H2SO4 to form paste. Paste is continuously applied to grid surface by belt paster. Pasted plates are flash-dried, then cut to cell width. Double-sided pasting ensures uniform thickness.

## 3. Quality Control

Paste density (g/cm3), paste penetration into grid, plate thickness uniformity, surface finish. Automated pasting at Chengguang with real-time thickness monitoring.
