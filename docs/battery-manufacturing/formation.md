# Formation Charging

## 1. Definition

Initial electrochemical charging that converts cured paste into active battery materials — PbO2 on positive plates and sponge lead on negative.

## 2. Process

Dry unformed batteries filled with electrolyte (specific gravity 1.050-1.100). DC current applied in multi-step profile over 18-48 hours. Positive plates convert from PbSO4/tribasic to PbO2 (dark brown). Negative plates convert to sponge Pb (gray). Temperature monitored throughout.

## 3. Quality Control

Formation efficiency (Ah in vs Ah out), final specific gravity, plate color uniformity, cell voltage consistency. Chengguang uses water-bath formation for temperature control.
