# C20 Capacity (Ampere-Hour)

## 1. Definition

C20 (20-hour rate) measures **ampere-hours (Ah)** a battery delivers at constant current over **20 hours** at **25degC**, until voltage reaches 10.5V. Example: 65Ah (C20) = delivers 3.25A for 20 hours.

## 2. Test Method

Battery discharged at C20 current (rated Ah / 20), maintained at 25degC +-2degC. Test ends at 10.5V. New battery must achieve >=95% of rated capacity.

## 3. Standards

IEC 60095-1, EN 50342-1, JIS D 5301, SAE J537. C20 is distinct from C5, C10, and RC ratings.

## 4. Practical Application

Ah rating helps estimate: runtime with engine off, battery size vs vehicle demand, suitability for deep-cycling.

## 6. FAQ

**Q: Difference between C20 and CCA?**

CCA = short-burst cold power. C20 = long-duration energy storage. Both important — CCA for starting, C20 for capacity.
