# Battery Testing

| Test | Measures | Key Standard |
|------|----------|-------------|
| [CCA](cca.md) | Cold starting power at -18degC | SAE J537 |
| [Reserve Capacity](reserve-capacity.md) | Runtime without alternator | SAE J537 |
| [C20 Capacity](c20-capacity.md) | Energy storage (Ah) | IEC 60095-1 |
| [Charge Acceptance](charge-acceptance.md) | Recharge speed | EN 50342-6 |

!!! warning "CCA Standards Are Not Interchangeable"
    A battery rated 500 CCA (SAE) may test 550 (EN) or 400 (JIS). Always check the standard.
